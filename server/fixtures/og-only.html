Weeknight Lemon Chicken

Serves 4 · Prep time: 10 min · Cook time: 20 min

Ingredients

4 chicken thighs
2 tbsp olive oil
3 garlic cloves, minced
juice of 1 lemon
1 tsp oregano
Salt to taste

Instructions

1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Sear 4 chicken thighs for 5 minutes per side.
3. Add the garlic, oregano and juice of 1 lemon, and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Season with salt to taste and serve.
